About this guide. This index follows The Complete Ethiopian Bible in English from Solomon’s Gate Press, using the 1–88 order of its accompanying audio edition. A number identifies a book; repeated audio tracks under one number are parts of that book, not extra entries. Titles and arrangements differ among Ethiopian and other Bible editions, so use this alongside the edition you are reading. The notes are brief reading companions, not a substitute for the text.

📜 Old Testament

Israel’s story: creation, ancestors, exodus, land, kingdoms, wisdom, prophets, exile, and return.

    ✝️ New Testament

    The four accounts of Jesus, Acts, letters to early Christian communities, and Revelation.
