🕎 Old Testament Era (Chronological Flow)

Primeval beginnings → patriarchs → Exodus → conquest → monarchy → exile → post-exile. Poetry & wisdom are placed near their historical settings; prophets sit where their ministries likely occurred.

    📜 Intertestamental / Second Temple Literature

    Works from the centuries around the close of the Hebrew Bible leading into the time of Jesus; includes deuterocanonical history and wisdom.

      ✝️ New Testament Era (Chronological Flow)

      Life of Jesus → Acts era → earliest letters → late letters → Revelation. Non-canonical Christian texts are placed near their traditional composition windows.
