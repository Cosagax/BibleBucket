Entire body including robe hem and staff in frame with ample transparent margin. Soft warm celestial rim light, muted moonlit shadows. Flowing natural narrow robe silhouette, no rectangular body, no outstretched arms. Isolated character only on genuinely transparent background, no scenery, no floor, no text, no halo, no star dots. This will be displayed small against a dark night sky. Reference attached image for character likeness and clothing only; exclude all interface elements.

Normal gameplay still requires the score goal. Escape or the explicit Skip ending button shows the final verse; Play Again returns to the intro.
